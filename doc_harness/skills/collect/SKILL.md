---
name: collect
description: Add files or public links to the active working folder.
---
Paste a .pdf, .docx, .md, .txt, or .html path or an http(s) URL directly into
the composer. Enter adds it; files are copied into inputs/ and URLs are
fetched during the build. Expert shortcut: /skill collect PATH_OR_URL.
The workflow must stop rather than omit an unreadable source.
